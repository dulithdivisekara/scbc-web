The Annual Pirith Chanting Ceremony will be held at the main assembly hall to invoke blessings upon all students sitting for the upcoming Advanced Level and Ordinary Level examinations. All parents, past pupils, and well-wishers are cordially invited to participate in this meritorious event. May the Triple Gems bless you!
