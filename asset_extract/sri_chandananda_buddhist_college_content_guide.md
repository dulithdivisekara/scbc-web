### PAGE: About Us
**Our Vision:**
"To create a healthy generation which faces the challenges of the modern world, practicing the values of Buddhist Philosophy."

**Our History:**
Sri Chandananda Buddhist College was established on January 4, 2006. The school was conceptualized and brought to life by our Founder and Executive Director, Most Ven. Dr. Godagama Mangala Thero. It is proudly named in honor of his respected teacher, the Most Ven. Palipana Sri Chandananda Mahanayake Thera, continuing a legacy of educational and spiritual excellence in Kandy. In 2020, we expanded our vision by opening a dedicated Girls' School section.

**Leadership:**
*   **Executive Director & Founder:** Most Ven. Dr. Godagama Mangala Thero
*   **Principal:** Ven. Godagama Dhammakiththi Thero
*   **Vice Principals:** Ven. Thulawala Dhammasiddi Thero & Mr. J.B. Dassanayaka

### PAGE: Academics
**Academic Structure:**
We offer comprehensive education from Grade 1 to Grade 13, preparing students for both Ordinary Level (O/L) and Advanced Level (A/L) examinations. Classes are conducted in both Sinhala and English mediums.

**Facilities & Global Learning:**
*   **Science & Tech:** Fully equipped IT laboratories, alongside specialized Biology, Chemistry, and Physics labs for our A/L students.
*   **Languages:** We prepare students for a globalized world by offering international language studies, including Chinese and Japanese.

### PAGE: Student Life
**Our Culture:**
Our school culture is deeply rooted in respect and mindfulness. Students greet one another with "May the triple gems bless you!" and address our educators as "Guruthuma" and "Guruthumi," fostering a highly disciplined and compassionate environment.
